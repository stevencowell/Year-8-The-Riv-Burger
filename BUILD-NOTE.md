# Build note — The Riv Burger

## Student promise

Students can move from the design situation to a planned, safely produced and evidence-based signature burger without relying on unexplained worksheets. Every module contains concise theory, five knowledge-check questions with section-specific feedback, and two autosaving written-evidence prompts.

## Ten-module map

1. The challenge
2. Safe food practices
3. Tools and workflow
4. Burger anatomy
5. Ingredients and agriculture
6. Develop your design
7. Garden to kitchen
8. Plan production
9. Produce and present
10. Evaluate and reflect

## Evidence and submission boundary

The folio gathers the twenty module responses, supports editing, print and JSON backup/restore. Storage is browser-local. Formal dates and submission arrangements remain teacher-controlled.

## Family-site decisions

- Sticky cream navigation, strong hero, numbered module cards and destination links follow the established family rhythm.
- The leaf, tomato and mustard palette gives the course a food-and-garden identity without breaking family structure.
- Main Menu links point to the established Industrial Arts Main Page.
