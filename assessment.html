Source-grounded student guide

Assessment

Design and produce a signature burger using locally grown produce and home-grown herbs, then communicate and evaluate your design process.

What you are being asked to do

The authorised school task describes a local small-business context and asks students to show how fresh, healthy and sustainable choices can form an appealing burger for young people.

  • Develop and communicate burger design options.
  • Select and justify a final design against criteria and constraints.
  • Prepare the required recipe, food order and workplan.
  • Produce and present the burger using safe food practices.
  • Evaluate the result and reflect on practical learning.

Use the design criteria as checkpoints

Product works

Stable, comfortable to eat, suitable portion, effective ingredient functions and safe production.

Product appeals

Intentional colour, aroma, flavour, texture, presentation and creative response to the brief.

Product is responsible

Evidence-based local, ethical and environmental choices with realistic use of time and resources.
