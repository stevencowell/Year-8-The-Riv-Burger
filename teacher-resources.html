Evidence, feedback and support

EvidenceLook forRespond / support
Theory and module workUnderstanding and use of key ideasReturn to the named section; model a stronger response
Project and folioDesign decisions, planning and refinementConference against criteria; give one clear next step
Practical observationSafety, workflow, skill, quality and clean-downPause, demonstrate and recheck before continuing

Support: model processes, use visual steps and word banks, chunk instructions, allow approved ways to show learning and plan accessible practical roles.

Practical controls: use current school procedures, allergy controls, inductions and supervision; approve recipes, ingredients, equipment and workplans before ordering or production.

Curriculum alignment

Current Stage 4 syllabus mapping

The program maps the published course to seven outcomes from the NSW Technology 7–8 Syllabus (2023) and the Food and agricultural practices focus-area content. It treats the ten-week order as a local teaching decision, not a syllabus-prescribed sequence.
