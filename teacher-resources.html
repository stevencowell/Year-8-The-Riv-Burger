Curriculum alignment

Current Stage 4 syllabus mapping

The program maps the published course to seven outcomes from the NSW Technology 7–8 Syllabus (2023) and the Food and agricultural practices focus-area content. It treats the ten-week order as a local teaching decision, not a syllabus-prescribed sequence.
