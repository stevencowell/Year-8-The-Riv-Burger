# The Riv Burger

A student course website for the Technology Mandatory Food and Agricultural Practices unit, **The Riv Burger**.

The course uses the NSW Technology 7–8 Syllabus (2023) and the authorised Riv Burger teacher program, folio and assessment sources. It is designed as a ten-module learning sequence with saved written evidence, knowledge checks, an assessment guide and a printable folio.

## Student privacy

No student submissions, names or private student photographs are included. Student writing saves only in the browser on the device being used; students must follow their teacher's directions for formal submission.

## Source boundary

Current syllabus requirements are separated from school unit details. Dates, lesson timing and submission locations remain teacher-controlled where the supplied sources do not establish a current value.
